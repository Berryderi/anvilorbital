Two founders, at the very start

We're two high school students in Ontario. We started on this on November 2, 2026, and we work on it alongside school.

Who does what

One of us builds the software. The other leads the non-technical side. We use AI coding and research tools, and we say where nobody has checked the results.

What we don't have

  • Spacecraft experience. Neither of us has built a spacecraft, a robot or space hardware. This is our first gap.
  • Customers. Nobody has used this and nobody has agreed to pay.
  • A company. None has been formed, and we've taken no investment. We're under 18, so an adult would need to sign any contract.

Who we'd like to hear from

Spacecraft engineers who can tell us what's wrong with our models, and people designing or running satellite fleets who think about end of life. Get in touch.
